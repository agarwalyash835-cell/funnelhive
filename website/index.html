FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I need a credit card for the trial?

No. Sign up and use every feature free for 14 days. Subscribe only if you want to keep going.

Do you take a cut of my sales?

Never. Your customers pay you directly through your own Razorpay or Stripe account. We don’t touch your revenue.

I’m not technical. Can I still use it?

Yes — that’s the point. Tell Hive AI what you want in plain English or Hinglish and it builds it. You can also edit everything with drag & drop.

Can I send WhatsApp messages from AIHive?

Yes. Connect the official WhatsApp Business (Cloud) API and send campaigns, reminders and automated follow-ups. Hive AI can even submit message templates to Meta for approval for you.

Can I use my own domain?

Yes — connect as many domains as you like. SSL certificates are issued automatically.

Will I get a GST invoice?

Yes, every payment comes with a downloadable GST invoice. Add your GSTIN in Billing to claim input tax credit. Your own customers get GST invoices automatically too.

What are AI credits?

Each month you get 1,500 AI credits. Building a full funnel with AI uses 5; an AI edit or Hive AI message uses 1. Everything else is unlimited.

What happens if I cancel?

You keep full access until the end of the period you paid for, and you can export all your data at any time.
