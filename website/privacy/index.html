Privacy policy

Last updated 6 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data Grovix Ventures Pvt Ltd (“we”, “us”) collects through AIHive (getaihive.in, app.getaihive.in and the pages our sellers publish), why we collect it and what you can do about it. It follows India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and the Information Technology Act, 2000 and its rules.

Who we are

AIHive is run by Grovix Ventures Pvt Ltd, Kolkata, West Bengal, India. For the personal data of sellers who use AIHive, we are the Data Fiduciary under the DPDP Act. Questions about this policy: support@theaihive.in.

This website

getaihive.in is an information page. It has no forms, sets no cookies and loads no analytics or advertising trackers. Our web server keeps standard technical logs (IP address, browser, the page requested and the time) for security and to keep the site running. The page’s fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address when you visit.

When you click “Start free trial” or “Log in” you move to app.getaihive.in, where the rest of this policy applies.

Data about sellers

When you create an AIHive account we collect:

  • Account details: your name, email address and password. The password is stored only as a secure hash, never in readable form.
  • Business and billing details: business name, country, state, GSTIN and billing address.
  • Payment records from Razorpay and Stripe: amounts, dates and payment status. We never see or store your full card number or UPI PIN.
  • What you create: funnels, pages, products, courses, messages, automations and files you upload.
  • Usage and technical data: which features you use, AI credits used, device and browser details, IP address and error logs.

Data about your customers

When people use the pages, checkouts, courses and communities you build on AIHive, we store what they submit on your behalf: for example name, email, phone number, form answers, orders, course progress and page visits.

For this data you are the Data Fiduciary and we are your Data Processor. We process it only to provide AIHive to you and on your instructions, never for our own marketing, and we help you answer your customers’ requests to access, correct or erase their data. You are responsible for having your customers’ consent, or another lawful reason, for what you collect and for the messages you send them.

How we use data

  • To run your account and deliver the features you use.
  • To bill you, issue invoices and meet tax and accounting law.
  • To keep AIHive secure, and to detect and stop fraud, spam and abuse.
  • To provide support and send service messages, such as billing notices, security alerts and changes to these policies.
  • To improve the product, using usage data that is aggregated wherever possible.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use your customers’ data to advertise to them.

Who we share it with

We share data only with providers that help us run AIHive (sub-processors), under contracts that limit them to doing that, and with authorities when the law requires it.

  • Hostinger: the server that runs AIHive and holds its database.
  • Wasabi: encrypted off-site backups, uploads and product files.
  • Cloudflare R2: protected course videos.
  • Razorpay and Stripe: subscription payments, and your customers’ payments into your own account.
  • Meta (WhatsApp Business Platform): WhatsApp messages you send.
  • Email delivery providers (Mailercloud, Resend or the SMTP server you connect): emails you send.
  • AI model providers: AI writing, image and other AI features, only for what you ask them to create.
  • Google: “Continue with Google” sign-in and Google Calendar, if you use them. Cloudflare Turnstile: bot checks on sign-up. Sentry: error reports. Better Stack: uptime monitoring, which receives no personal data.

Some of these providers process data outside India. When they do, we use providers bound by contract to protect it, and we follow any transfer restrictions notified under the DPDP Act.

Cookies

The AIHive app uses your browser’s storage to keep you signed in and remember your settings. These are needed for the app to work. Pages built by sellers may load the Meta Pixel or Google Analytics if the seller turns them on; by default these load only after the visitor accepts the page’s cookie notice.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account we delete its data, except invoices and payment records, which tax law requires us to keep for 8 years. Deleted data also leaves our backups over time: daily backups on our server are kept for 7 days and encrypted off-site backups for 90 days.

Security and breaches

Data travels over HTTPS, passwords are hashed, payment and messaging keys are encrypted at rest, and two-step login is available on every account. If a breach affects personal data, we will contain it, tell the affected people without delay what happened and what they can do, and report it to the Data Protection Board of India and CERT-In as the law requires.

Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you can:

  • ask what personal data we hold about you and how we use it;
  • correct, complete or update it;
  • have it erased, unless the law requires us to keep it;
  • withdraw your consent at any time, as easily as you gave it;
  • nominate someone to exercise these rights if you die or cannot act yourself; and
  • complain to our grievance officer, and then to the Data Protection Board of India.

Sellers can export, correct and delete their data from Settings in the app. For anything else, write to support@theaihive.in. If you are a customer of a seller, contact that seller first; we will help them answer you.

Children

AIHive is for people aged 18 or over. Sellers must not use it to collect children’s data without verifiable consent from a parent or guardian. We do not track children or show them targeted advertising.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a way that affects you, we will tell you by email or in the app before the change takes effect. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.

Grievance officer
