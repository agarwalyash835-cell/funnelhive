## Short description (max 80 characters)

Your courses, community and certificates — secure, private and always with you.

## Full description (max 4,000 characters)

AiHive Academy is the members' app for courses and coaching programmes. If you've joined a course, a cohort or a coaching programme from a creator or institute that runs its academy on AiHive, this is where you learn: your lessons, your progress, your community and your certificates, all in one place.

LEARN AT YOUR OWN PACE
• Watch video lessons, listen to audio lessons and read PDFs and notes, all inside the app
• Pick up exactly where you left off: your progress and last lesson are saved automatically
• See how far you've come in every course, lesson by lesson
• Lessons that open on a schedule unlock on their own, so you always know what's next

QUIZZES AND CERTIFICATES
• Test yourself with quizzes at the end of lessons
• Finish every lesson and pass every quiz to earn your certificate of completion
• Keep all your certificates in one place, ready to share

ASK, DISCUSS, GROW TOGETHER
• Ask questions under any lesson and get answers from your teacher
• Join your academy's community spaces: post, comment, like and learn from other members
• Earn points for helping others, climb the levels and see yourself on the leaderboard
• Get notified when someone replies to you (you can turn alerts off any time)

A TUTOR THAT KNOWS YOUR COURSE
Where your academy has it switched on, the built-in tutor answers your questions using your course's own lessons, so you get help that matches what you're actually being taught.

PRIVATE AND PROTECTED
Your teacher's work is protected, and so is your account:
• Screenshots and screen recording are blocked inside the app
• Lessons stream securely and can't be downloaded or copied
• Sign in with a one-time code sent to your email. There's no password to remember or leak.
• Your account works on a limited number of devices, so your access stays yours

MANY ACADEMIES, ONE APP
Learning from more than one creator? Add each academy to the app and switch between them in a tap. Opening an academy link from your email or WhatsApp takes you straight to it.

IN YOUR LANGUAGE
Use the app in English, Hindi, Hinglish, Marathi, Gujarati, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Punjabi or Odia, and switch any time.

HOW TO GET STARTED
1. Buy or join a course from a creator or institute that uses AiHive.
2. Open the app and add your academy (or tap the academy link you received).
3. Enter the email you used when you joined, type the code we send you, and start learning.

AiHive Academy is free to download. Courses are sold by the individual creators and institutes, not in this app; you'll need access to a course from one of them to sign in.

Questions about a course? Contact the creator or institute you joined. Questions about the app? Write to us at the developer email on this page.
